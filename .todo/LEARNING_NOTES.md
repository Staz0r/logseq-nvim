# Learning Notes

Record reusable engineering lessons discovered during implementation or review.
Keep temporary debugging transcripts and task status in their proper locations.

## Entry template

### <<YYYY-MM-DD — concise title>>

- **Context:** <<task or incident>>
- **Finding:** <<what was learned>>
- **Rule:** <<what future work should do>>
- **Evidence:** <<file, test, documentation, issue, or PR>>

Promote durable architecture decisions to `docs/DECISIONS.md`, testing rules to
`docs/TESTING_GUIDE.md`, and active work to `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
